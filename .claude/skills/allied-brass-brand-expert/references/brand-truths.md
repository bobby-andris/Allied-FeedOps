# Allied Brass Brand Truths — Verified Claims

Every claim below has been verified through product catalog data, company records,
customer reviews, competitor analysis, or material science documentation.

## Material: Solid Brass

- **Claim**: Products built from solid brass, not zinc alloy (zamak)
- **Source**: Amazon listings, company About page, product specifications
- **Verification**: "Towel bars are made of solid brass to ensure the product will not rust
  and the designer finishes will provide a lifetime of beauty" — Amazon store listing
- **Material science**: Brass is stronger, more malleable, and more corrosion-resistant than
  zinc alloy. Zinc cracks under stress; brass bends. Zinc corrodes in humidity; brass develops
  a protective patina.
- **Competitor reality**: Moen, Delta use zinc/zamak bodies with plated finishes.
  Kingston Brass also claims solid brass. Kohler uses mixed materials.
