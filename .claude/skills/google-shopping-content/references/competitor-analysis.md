# Competitive Positioning — Allied Brass vs Market

**Source:** Phase 17 competitive gap analysis, Google Ads search term data
**Last updated:** 2026-02-21

## Competitive Landscape

### Kingston Brass (Primary Competitor)
- **Distribution:** Home Depot (DA 90+), Amazon (DA 96), Wayfair, direct site
- **Material:** Solid brass AND zinc alloy (mixed quality across line)
- **Finishes:** ~12 finishes (vs Allied Brass 28)
- **Pricing:** 50-100% below Allied Brass for equivalent sizes
- **Title formula:** `[Brand] [Size]-In. [Product Type], [Finish]` — clean, attribute-rich
- **Weakness:** Generic descriptions, no lifestyle images, inconsistent material quality
- **Why they win clicks:** Platform authority (homedepot.com DA) + well-structured titles

### Moen (Mass Market)
- **Distribution:** Home Depot, Lowe's, Amazon
- **Material:** Chrome-plated steel, zinc alloy (NOT solid brass)
- **Title formula:** `[Collection] [Size] [Product Type]` — relies on brand recognition
- **Lesson:** Moen's collection-forward approach works because "Moen Align" IS a search term. "Allied Brass Skyline" is NOT. Do not emulate Moen's title strategy.

### Delta (Mass Market)
- **Distribution:** Home Depot, Lowe's, Amazon
- **Material:** Plastic core components, chrome-plated steel
- **Pricing:** Budget-friendly, undercuts Allied Brass significantly
- **Not a direct competitor** on quality — competes on price in commodity categories

### Signature Hardware (Niche Premium)
- **Distribution:** Direct site (signaturehardware.com)
- **Material:** Mixed — some solid brass, some plated
- **Title formula:** `[Finish] [Size] [Product Type] | [Collection]` — finish-first
- **Includes "ADA Compliant" in grab bar titles** — matches safety searches
- **Pricing:** $70-$350 for grab bars — comparable to Allied Brass

### Barclay Products (Niche Decorative)
- **Distribution:** Direct site, specialty retailers
- **Relevance:** Wins "decorative grab bar" terms with lower DA than Allied Brass
- **Lesson:** A DTC brand CAN win decorative terms if the title contains "decorative"

## Allied Brass Differentiation Points

### Material Advantage (STRONGEST differentiator)
- Allied Brass: **Solid brass construction** throughout
- Kingston Brass: Mix of solid brass and zinc alloy
- Moen: Chrome-plated steel
- Delta: Plastic cores with plating
- **Use in every title:** "Solid Brass" is factual and differentiating

### Finish Variety (UNIQUE differentiator)
- Allied Brass: **28 finishes** including specialty options
- Kingston Brass: ~12 finishes
- Moen: 4-6 finishes per product
- **Specialty finishes competitors DON'T offer:**
  - Unlacquered Brass (develops patina)
  - Venetian Bronze
  - Antique Copper
  - Satin Chrome
- Finish-specific searches have 2-3x higher CTR than generic searches

### Lifestyle Images (IMPLEMENTED advantage)
- Allied Brass: Lifestyle images via Gemini Imagen in Google Sheets feed
- Kingston Brass (via Home Depot): Mostly product-only photography
- **15-25% CTR improvement** documented for home decor with lifestyle images

### Collection Coordination (DESIGN advantage)
- Allied Brass: 41 named collections spanning all bathroom categories
- Competitors: Limited collection depth — most only in 1-2 categories
- **Cross-sell signal:** "Coordinates with Waverly Place towel bar, robe hook, and soap dish"

## Structural Disadvantages (Cannot Fix via Feed)

### Domain Authority Gap
- alliedbrass.com (Shopify): Estimated DA 25-40
- homedepot.com: DA 90+
- amazon.com: DA 96
- **Implication:** Allied Brass must have BETTER feed content to compensate

### Seller Ratings
- Competitors via Amazon/Home Depot: Automatic star ratings in Shopping
- Allied Brass: No seller ratings confirmed (needs MC diagnostic)
- **17-30% CTR lift** from seller ratings

### Review Count
- Kingston Brass via Home Depot: 100-300+ reviews per product
- Allied Brass: Review count unverified (likely lower)
- **Products with 0 reviews are algorithmically disadvantaged**

## Competitive Strategy Summary

1. **Win on specialty intent** — Finish-specific, decorative, designer searches where big-box retailers have weak inventory
2. **Material differentiation in every listing** — "Solid Brass" in title, material comparison in description
3. **Finish variety as moat** — 28 finishes vs competitor 4-12 is a genuine advantage
4. **Lifestyle images** — Already implemented, competitors lack this
5. **Don't compete on generic commodity terms** — "towel bar" will be won by Home Depot. "Unlacquered brass 24-inch solid brass towel bar" is winnable.
