## Lighting and Photography Notes

From `FINISH_LIGHTING` in `lifestyle_images.py` — used for AI image generation:

- **Reflective finishes** (Polished Chrome, Polished Brass, Polished Nickel): Need bright, diffuse lighting to show mirror quality without blown highlights
- **Brushed/satin finishes** (Satin Nickel, Satin Brass, Satin Chrome): Directional lighting reveals texture grain
- **Dark finishes** (Oil Rubbed Bronze, Matte Black): Need warm directional lighting to prevent flat appearance
- **Statement colors**: Even studio lighting for accurate, saturated color reproduction
- **Living finish** (Unlacquered Brass): Natural warm lighting to show character without over-emphasizing variation
