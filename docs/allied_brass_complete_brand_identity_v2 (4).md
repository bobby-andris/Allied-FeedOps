# Allied Brass Brand Identity

## **Our Competitive Edge**

Our products blend form and function seamlessly. By offering aesthetically pleasing yet incredibly practical solutions such as the retractable garment rod, ceiling hung mirror, rollerless toilet paper holder, grab bars, and more, we ensure that our customers never have to compromise on style for utility.

Our product designs, from the clean lines of a garment rod to the refined elegance of our grab bars, set us apart from the competition. Unlike typical utilitarian offerings in the market, we have successfully merged functionality with sophistication, creating solutions that appeal to customers with a discerning eye for design.

**Innovation That Solves Real Problems:** Our rollerless toilet paper holder eliminates mechanical failure points through gravity-operated design. Our ladder towel bars maximize storage in minimal wall space. Our combination products integrate multiple functions without compromising aesthetics. This is 65 years of American engineering applied to everyday challenges—creating products that work better and look better.

### **Exceptional Product Finishes**

What truly distinguishes us in the market is our commitment to variety and consistency in product finishes. Every single product in our catalog is available in an impressive range of over 25 finishes. This breadth and depth of options is virtually unparalleled in our industry.

While other manufacturers offer a variety of finishes, the reality often falls short of the promise. Many products are only available in a handful of finishes, and there's frequently a lack of consistency across product lines. This inconsistency often leaves customers compromising on their ideal style to settle for what's available.

**Complete Customization Capability:** At Allied Brass, we transform this industry limitation into our greatest strength. Our commitment to offering ALL products in ALL finishes, across ALL 42+ designer collections (e.g., Dottingham, Pacific Beach, Pipeline, Carolina) gives our customers an unprecedented level of flexibility and customization. This empowers them to create a home that genuinely reflects their unique style, without the need for compromise.

**Our Specialization Advantage:** Allied Brass focuses exclusively on decorative bathroom and kitchen hardware—and this specialization allows us to excel where others cannot. While companies that spread across multiple product categories offer limited options in each, our focused expertise enables complete design coordination across towel bars, toilet paper holders, mirrors, grab bars, and cabinet hardware. Customers can coordinate their entire space seamlessly, thanks to our unmatched variety and consistency in finishes within our specialty.

This represents our dedication to customer satisfaction and embodies our philosophy: "Your style, your finish, your choice."
