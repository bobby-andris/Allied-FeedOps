### **Exceptional Product Finishes**

What truly distinguishes us in the market is our commitment to variety and consistency in product finishes. Every single product in our catalog is available in an impressive range of over 28 finishes. This breadth and depth of options is virtually unparalleled in our industry.

While industry giants like Kohler, Delta, Signature Hardware, and American Standard offer a variety of finishes, the reality often falls short of the promise. Many of their products are only available in a handful of finishes, and there's frequently a lack of consistency across product lines. This lack of uniformity often leaves customers compromising on their ideal style to settle for what's available.

At Allied Brass, we turn this industry norm on its head. Our commitment to offering ALL products in ALL finishes, across ALL designer collections (e.g., Dottingham, Pacific Beach) gives our customers an unprecedented level of flexibility and customization. This empowers them to create a home that genuinely reflects their unique style, without the need for compromise.

Customers can explore endless combinations and coordinate their entire bathroom or kitchen decor seamlessly, thanks to our unmatched variety and consistency in finishes. This is a testament to our dedication to customer satisfaction and an embodiment of our philosophy: "Your style, your finish, your choice."
