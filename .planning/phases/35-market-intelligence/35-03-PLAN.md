  - Bottom-left: "Dogs" (red)
- Custom Tooltip showing: group name, ROAS, revenue, spend, trend, quadrant

- When `dimmed` prop is true, apply `opacity-40 transition-opacity duration-300` to the chart container
- Wrap in `ResponsiveContainer` with height={500}
- Format axis labels: ROAS as "X.Xx", Revenue as "$XX,XXX"

**Important Recharts ScatterChart notes:**
- Data format: array of objects `[{ customLabel0, roas, revenue, spend, quadrant, trend }]`
- ZAxis controls bubble size; set `type="number"` and `range={[minPx, maxPx]}`
- To make bubbles clickable, attach onClick to the Scatter component or use Cell with cursor="pointer"
- ReferenceLine: `<ReferenceLine x={medianRoas} stroke="#9ca3af" strokeDasharray="5 5" />`
