    SKU name: `<span className="font-medium text-sm w-28 flex-shrink-0">{master_sku}</span>`

    Platform badges: `flex gap-1 flex-shrink-0` — three small badges in sequence.
    Quality score: `<span className="text-sm font-medium w-8 text-right flex-shrink-0 {scoreColor}">{avg_quality_score ?? '—'}</span>` — use green ≥80, yellow ≥60, red otherwise.
