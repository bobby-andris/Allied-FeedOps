## Decisions Made
- Composite weights 0.60/0.40 (attribute/specificity) per plan specification
- Model number short-circuits to 1.0 (strongest purchase intent signal)
- Unknown TF-IDF terms get 80% of max IDF (encourages scoring novel but specific queries)
- Lazy initialization with threading.Lock for thread-safe singleton
- Word-boundary matching for 2-3 character finish codes to prevent false positives (e.g., "PB" not matching "capable")
