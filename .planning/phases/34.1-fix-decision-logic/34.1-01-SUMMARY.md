## Accomplishments
- Wasted spend terms (0 conversions, >$5) now always get 'block' (HIGH) or 'constrain' (MEDIUM/LOW), never 'promote'
- Impact for wasted spend is cost-based ($4-$8 for $8 spend), no longer $0
- CPC fit scoring uses asymmetric penalty: cheap CPC is neutral, expensive CPC penalizes
- All verdict text is prescriptive ("Block -- spent $10 with zero conversions") not descriptive ("fits LOW distribution")
- 8 new tests covering all 4 bug fixes, all passing
