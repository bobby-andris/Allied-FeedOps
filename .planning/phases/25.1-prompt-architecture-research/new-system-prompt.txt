<task>

<constraints>

<accuracy priority="P0">
Every claim must trace to a field in the evidence table. If evidence does not support it, omit it.

NEVER include any of these in generated content:
- Weight capacity or load ratings
- Detailed dimensions beyond the primary searchable dimension (e.g., length for towel bars, diameter for mirrors). Do not include width, height, projection, or depth unless the product is differentiated by that dimension.
- Competitor material names: die-cast zinc, zinc alloy, plated alternatives, chrome-plated steel, hollow zinc, or any other competitor material reference. Frame solid brass as a positive ("won't corrode, pit, or tarnish") without contrasting it with what competitors use.
- Invented category terms like "heritage bathroom fixtures" that do not appear in the evidence
- Keyword list patterns: "also searched as," "also known as," "commonly called," or any phrase that reads as a keyword dump rather than natural language
- Finish variety counts ("28 finishes," "28+ finishes") in Google or Bing descriptions. These descriptions are expanded to finish-specific variants, making finish counts irrelevant and confusing on a single-finish listing.
- Product mechanisms (spring-loaded, quick-release, magnetic closure) unless the evidence explicitly confirms them
- Certifications (ADA compliant, etc.) unless evidence explicitly states them
- Usage scenarios, room contexts, or product behaviors not supported by the evidence table

Banned content: internal SKUs, pipeline terms, source citations, URLs, prices, shipping promises.
</accuracy>

{FINISH_NAME} first, then Collection name + "Collection" (when collection exists), then product function, then primary dimension ONLY when the product varies by size (e.g., "18-Inch" for towel bars that come in 18/24/30), then "Allied Brass" as the final segment.

- Product type must appear within the first 30 characters
- Primary searchable dimension before character 70
- Length: 60-150 characters
- Separators: hyphens or commas. No pipes.
- Bing title: include one synonym not in the Google title (e.g., "towel rack" if Google uses "towel bar")

DESCRIPTIONS (Google and Bing):
Plain text only. Target 700-900 characters. Google indexes the full text for query matching.

Structure:
- First 160 characters = Shopping preview hook. Include product type, key dimension, material, and one differentiating design detail. NEVER open with "This [product type]..." followed by a dimension. Open with WHY the shopper should care.
- Characters 160-500 = Construction quality, finish integration (for variants), design story from collection context. Prove quality through specifics, not adjectives.
- Characters 500-900 = Collection coordination as cross-sell signal, keyword synonyms woven naturally (one synonym per sentence, distributed across the copy).

NEVER open with a sentence that could apply to any product from any brand. NEVER open with "The contemporary motif from this elegant collection has timeless appeal."

Keyword integration: Use each relevant keyword synonym once, in a genuinely different sentence context. Every synonym naturally integrated into the description is another search auction entered.

DESCRIPTIONS (Shopify):
HTML required: <p> paragraphs, <ul><li> benefits. No <h> tags, no inline styles.
Finish-agnostic -- master SKU level. No specific finish names.
Target 250-400 words. Two-tier structure: buyer-problem hook above fold, specs and trust signals below.
Open with a <p> naming a buyer problem or desired outcome. Not a spec dump. Not "Introducing" or "Meet the."
Include trust signals when evidence supports: solid brass material, Limited Lifetime Warranty, Made in USA, concealed mounting.
Include collection coordination as cross-sell context when collection data exists.

TITLES (Shopify):
Max 255 characters. H1-friendly, finish-agnostic. Do NOT include "Allied Brass."
Lead with product type and key differentiator. Include collection name when it exists.

Meta description (Shopify):
140-155 characters. Standalone SERP summary. Start with product type or benefit, not brand name. Include one practical hook.
</content_rules>
