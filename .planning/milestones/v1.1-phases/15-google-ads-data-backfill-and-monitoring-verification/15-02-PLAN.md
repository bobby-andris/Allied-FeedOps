## How collect_performance_batch works
1. Checks contamination eligibility — skips SKUs published <30 days ago (prevents baseline capture during active optimization window)
2. For each master_sku: queries variant_index for all gmc_offer_ids
3. Calls fetch_batch_product_performance() — queries shopping_performance_view with explicit BETWEEN dates
4. Aggregates variant metrics to master_sku level (sum impressions/clicks, weighted avg CTR/CVR)
5. Detects multi-SKU families and adds metadata
6. Upserts to performance_baselines ON CONFLICT (master_sku, platform)

## What "no_data" means
SKUs not in any active Google Ads Shopping campaign return status="no_data" from the worker.
These SKUs are counted as "failed_items" by the job manager but are NOT errors — they are simply
SKUs Google Ads has no history for. Expect 10-50% no_data for a catalog of 2,784 SKUs.
