# v1.3a Content Generation Excellence — Milestone Audit

**Milestone Goal:** Fix content quality at its source — repair GPT-5.2 integration bugs, establish gold standards and a differentiation-first quality rubric, rewrite prompts as creative briefs, wire all 8 runtime configs, and validate with human evaluation across 10 representative SKUs.

**Audited:** 2026-02-24
**Status:** GAPS FOUND — 4 EVAL requirements unsatisfied

---

## Executive Summary

v1.3a achieved its **infrastructure** goals completely: GPT-5.2 bugs fixed (5/5), gold standards loaded (15+ across 10 categories), quality rubric rewritten to 10-criterion model, SYSTEM_PROMPT rebuilt as creative brief, all 8 skills wired, category guidance expanded to 24 entries. The foundation is solid.

v1.3a **did not achieve** its **evaluation** goals: After 2 rounds of human evaluation and 6+ iterations of prompt work (Phases 25, 25.1, 25.2, 25.3), content quality improved significantly (titles: 0→4/10, differentiation: 3→5-10/10) but did not reach the 8/10 threshold for titles or descriptions. The remaining failures are specific and well-diagnosed (competitor material naming, excessive dimensions, weight capacity inclusion, product type misidentification) — these are content rules, not architectural issues.

The v2 per-platform prompt architecture (Phase 25.2/25.3) is complete but has never been activated in production (`FEEDOPS_PROMPT_VERSION` defaults to `"v1"`). Phase 25.3 (prompt rewrite from human feedback) has 0/4 plans executed. The evaluation iteration cycle (Round 3) was never run.

**21 of 25 requirements are satisfied.** The 4 unsatisfied requirements (EVAL-03, EVAL-04, EVAL-05, EVAL-06) all relate to the human evaluation threshold not being met and the test batch not being published.
